# 2026.325.0959 — feat(resolve): render-prep asks the content kinds; imported candies carry the skills they own

## Summary

The build plugin's half of opencharly/sdk#598:

- **`ownedContentSource`** builds render-prep's way to reach the content kinds, once per build resolve:
  - the kind words come from the host's `validate-word-sets` leg (`content_kinds`, a registry fact only the host holds);
  - `Ask` is a peer `InvokeProvider` of `kind:<word>` `ops.OpContent`, carrying the request verbatim.

  It is wired into the root render-prep (`Generator.Content`) and threaded to the namespace fill. The resolves that render nothing (`build:project`, tolerant validate) pass the zero source, which asks no kind.
- **The namespace fold** projects candies through `deploykit.ProjectCandy` and merges ownership with `deploykit.UnionOwnedSkills`. It used to copy `RawCandyPair` alone and skip any candy another scope had recorded first, so an imported skill-owning candy read as empty and validate refused it. Measured on pod-kde-cstream importing distro-arch: `arch-skills` was recorded by a scope holding none of its 8 skill nodes, so the `arch` namespace that owns 6 of them was skipped.
